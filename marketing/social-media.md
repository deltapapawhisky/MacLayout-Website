## Mastodon / Bluesky Post

I just launched MacLayout, a native macOS app I've been building for the past 3 months.

The problem it solves: every time you dock or undock your MacBook, all your windows end up in a pile and you spend minutes dragging them back to where they belong.

MacLayout lets you save your window arrangements as named layouts and restore them with one click, a keyboard shortcut, or automatically when your display configuration changes.

Key features:
- Save unlimited named layouts
- Auto-restore on display change (dock/undock triggers)
- App launch triggers (position windows when an app opens)
- Full macOS Spaces/Desktops support
- Global keyboard shortcuts
- Native Swift app, runs in the menu bar

It's different from Rectangle/Magnet — those snap individual windows, MacLayout remembers and restores your entire workspace.

$19.99 one-time purchase. 14-day free trial. No subscription.
