## Maker's Comment

Hey Product Hunt!

I'm the solo developer behind MacLayout, and I built it to solve a problem I deal with every single day.

**The problem:** I work from a MacBook Pro and dock it to an ultrawide monitor at my desk. Every time I connect or disconnect, all my windows end up piled on top of each other in the wrong places. I'd spend 5 minutes dragging everything back — Slack on the left, browser on the right, terminal in the corner, Messages on the laptop screen. Do that 2-3 times a day and it adds up fast.

**What MacLayout does:** You arrange your windows the way you like them, save that arrangement as a named layout, and restore it anytime. But the real magic is auto-triggers — MacLayout can detect when your display setup changes (you dock or undock your laptop) and automatically restore the right layout. No clicks needed.

**What makes it different from Rectangle, Magnet, etc.:** Those tools snap individual windows to positions (left half, right third, etc.), which is great. MacLayout does something different — it remembers your *entire* workspace state and brings it all back at once. Think of it as workspace snapshots vs. window snapping. They're complementary tools, actually.

**A few things I'm proud of:**
- Full macOS Spaces/Desktops support — it remembers which Space each window is on
- App launch triggers — automatically position an app's windows when it opens
- It's a native Swift app. No Electron. Runs in your menu bar and uses ~15MB of memory.
- One-time purchase ($19.99). No subscription for a utility app.

There's a 14-day free trial so you can make sure it works with your setup. I'm here all day to answer questions and take feedback.

Thank you for checking it out!
